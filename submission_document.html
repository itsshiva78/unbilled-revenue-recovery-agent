1. Frontier Capability Spotted & Caveat Designed Around

Capability Spotted: Multi-Source Model Context Protocol (MCP) Tool Orchestration with Structured Extraction and Legal Grounding (Groq / Llama-3.3-70B & Gemini). Early 2025 marked the convergence of open MCP server standards and lightning-fast LPU inference, allowing autonomous agents to query heterogeneous enterprise data (Google Meet, Gmail metadata, Jira task logs, and Harvest timesheets) without custom brittle connectors.

The Critical Caveat (The False-Positive Trap): Raw LLMs possess an acute people-pleasing bias and lack legal boundaries. If an unconstrained AI scans company communications, it attempts to bill clients for internal all-hands retrospectives, pre-sales capability pitches, personal dentist appointments, and pro-bono warranty defect remediation. Sending automated invoices for non-billable tasks destroys client trust and creates legal disputes.

The Engineering Guardrail: We built a 3-Stage Contract-Scope Classifier Guardrail:

  • Stage 1 (Deterministic Pre-Filter): Instantly eliminates internal team meetings, personal calendar events, and prospective client pitches at zero API token cost.
  • Stage 2 (Contract SOW Grounding): Submits candidate work to Groq's llama-3.3-70b-versatile with the client's executed SOW. The model must cite the verbatim clause (e.g. SOW §4.2) and extract textual evidence.
  • Stage 3 (Confidence Routing): High confidence (≥ 0.85) is approved for invoicing; moderate confidence (0.60–0.84) routes to PM review; low confidence (< 0.60) is automatically discarded.
Verification Proof: Tested against 4 rigorous negative controls (Meridian Sprint Retro, Dentist OOO, BioGen Pitch, RetailPulse Warranty Defect). 100% were cleanly rejected with zero false positive billable line items generated.

2. The ICP Operational Pain Point & Strategic Pairing

The ICP: 50 to 300 person service agencies, product studios, and digital consultancies in North America and MENA billing $150 to $250/hour.

The Pain Point ($1.56M Leaked Profit): In consulting firms, engineers and designers prioritize shipping over timesheet paperwork. When clients request emergency off-hours database migrations, weekend architecture reviews, or ad-hoc webhook integrations, staff jump on calls and write code—but routinely forget to enter them into Harvest or Toggl. At $200/hr, missing just 2 hours per staff member weekly leaks $1.04M to $1.56M in pure bottom-line profit every single year.

Why This Pairing Wins: While most applicants build generic lead-reply bots or meeting summarizers, unbilled recovery targets pure cash flow already earned. It provides immediate, mathematically verifiable ROI to agency founders without requiring behavior changes from creative teams.
